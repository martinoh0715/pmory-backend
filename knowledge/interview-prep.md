# PM Interview Preparation for Emory Students

Guidance for Product Management interviews at tech companies.

## Behavioral Interviews

- Use the STAR method: Situation, Task, Action, Result
- Prepare quantifiable examples of impact from coursework, internships, and leadership
- Show analytical thinking and user empathy in every story
- Demonstrate cross-functional collaboration even from academic group projects

## Product Design Questions

- Use the CIRCLES framework: Comprehend the situation, Identify the customer, Report customer needs, Cut through prioritization, List solutions, Evaluate tradeoffs, Summarize your recommendation
- Always start with the user and their pain points before jumping to solutions
- Discuss tradeoffs explicitly — PMs are evaluated on judgment, not just creativity

## Resume Tips for PM Roles

- Highlight data analysis projects, leadership roles, and cross-functional collaboration from your Emory experience
- Quantify outcomes where possible (users reached, revenue impact, efficiency gains)
- Include product-adjacent work: case competitions, consulting projects, apps built, user research conducted
- Tailor bullets to show customer empathy, prioritization, and shipping outcomes

## Common PM Interview Categories

- Product sense / design: "How would you improve X product?"
- Execution / prioritization: "What would you build first and why?"
- Analytical / estimation: Market sizing, metric diagnosis
- Behavioral: Leadership, conflict, failure, influence without authority
- Technical fluency: Explain how you'd work with engineers; basic system design awareness

## Emory-Specific Interview Advice

- Leverage Goizueta case interview skills for structured product thinking
- Reference specific Emory courses (MKT 347, ISOM 352) when discussing your preparation
- Connect campus projects to real product decisions you have made or would make
