# PM Career Tips for Emory Students

Practical career guidance for breaking into Product Management.

## Getting Started

- Build a small product project: app, website, or campus initiative with measurable users or outcomes
- Conduct user interviews — even 5 conversations teach more than reading alone
- Learn SQL and basic analytics; ISOM 352 is an excellent starting point at Emory
- Follow PM content: Lenny's Podcast, Product School, and company PM blogs

## Networking

- Connect with Emory alumni in PM roles on LinkedIn
- Attend product meetups in Atlanta and virtual PM communities
- Ask for informational interviews — prepare specific questions about their path from school to PM

## Internship and New Grad Paths

- Target APM (Associate Product Manager) programs and rotational programs
- Consider product-adjacent roles: product analyst, UX researcher, business analyst, consulting — as stepping stones
- PM internships often recruit in fall; start preparing resume and stories early

## Skills to Develop Outside the Classroom

- Figma for wireframing and prototyping
- Jira or Linear for agile workflow understanding
- Google Analytics or Mixpanel for product metrics
- Notion for PRDs and documentation

## What Recruiters Look For

- Evidence you can identify user problems and prioritize solutions
- Comfort with ambiguity and structured thinking
- Ability to communicate with technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Curiosity about products you use daily — always have thoughtful product opinions ready
