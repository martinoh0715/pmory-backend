# Emory Student Advantages for Product Management

Emory University students, especially those at Goizueta Business School, are well positioned for Product Management roles in tech.

## Why Emory Students Fit PM

- Strong business foundation from Goizueta Business School
- Analytical thinking developed through rigorous coursework
- Leadership experience from campus programs and organizations
- Network of successful alumni in the technology industry
- Case study methodology that mirrors PM problem-solving and structured thinking

## How to Position Your Emory Background

- Highlight quantitative coursework and data projects on your resume
- Emphasize cross-functional team projects and leadership roles
- Connect Goizueta case analysis skills to product sense and prioritization
- Reference specific clubs, hackathons, or product-building experiences at Emory
