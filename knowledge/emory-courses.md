# Emory Courses for Aspiring Product Managers

Curated Goizueta / Emory coursework for students targeting Product Management roles.
Source: Emory courses catalog export (September 2026). Titles match the official catalog.

## Core foundation courses

Build business fluency PMs use every day (metrics, customers, process, leadership).

- **ACT 200: Accounting: The Language of Business** — Read income statements, balance sheets, and cash flows so you can discuss unit economics and financial impact with stakeholders.
- **FIN 320: Corporate Finance** — Financial management, capital allocation, and ROI thinking for business cases and prioritization.
- **MKT 340: Marketing Management** — Introductory marketing: customer needs, positioning, and go-to-market fundamentals.
- **ISOM 351: Process & Systems Management** — Analyze, run, and improve operations and workflows — core to shipping and scaling products.
- **OAM 330: Organization & Management** — Applied behavioral science and how organizations actually get work done.
- **OAM 331: Strategic Management** — “Big picture” strategy: competitive advantage, industry structure, and why some companies win.
- **BUS 350: Data and Decision Analytics** — Manipulate real business data and use models for predictive, data-based decisions.
- **BUS 360: Design Thinking** — Human-centered methods to discover needs and design useful products, services, and experiences.

## Highest-priority PM electives

If you have limited elective slots, start here.

- **MKT 347: Product and Brand Management** — Closest classroom analog to the PM role: developing, managing, and evolving products and brands.
- **MKT 345: Advanced Marketing Strategy** — Segmentation, positioning, and implementing marketing strategy end to end.
- **MKT 342: Marketing Analytics** — Marketplace data and analysis tools that guide product and marketing decisions.
- **MKT 443: Monetization & Pricing Strategy** — Hands-on pricing tactics and monetization strategy (critical PM skill).
- **ISOM 352: Applied Data Analytics with Coding** — Python + data skills applied to marketing, finance, healthcare, and sports problems.
- **ISOM 456: Intro. to Business Data Analytics** — Systematic use of instrumented business data for better decisions.
- **ISOM 454: Data Science for Business** — Modern data science / AI concepts with coding for business problems.
- **ISOM 356: Think.Code.Make** — Coding, programming, and systems-design thinking — “how to think” like builders.

## Product, growth, and customer depth (Marketing)

- **MKT 346: Consumer Behavior** — Customer psychology, needs, and relationships — strong prep for product sense and user empathy.
- **MKT 343: Digital & Social Media Strategy** — Digital media landscape and marketing strategy in online channels.
- **MKT 441: Ideation** — Creative ideation tools and methods that fuel innovation.
- **MKT 442: Marketing and Consulting Practicum** — Build a strategic marketing plan for a real client (experiential).
- **MKT 462: Customer Lifetime Valuation** — Measure and manage customer value across industries.

## Digital products, platforms, and analytics (ISOM)

- **ISOM 355: Appcology: New Commerce Infrastructure** — Emerging apps/services, software ecosystems, and commerce interactions.
- **ISOM 450: Foundations of Digital Enterp.** — How personal, business, and government interactions move into digital environments.
- **ISOM 475: Data Visualization** — Build clear visualizations and dashboards that communicate data relationships.
- **ISOM 358: Advanced Decision Analytics** — Hands-on decision analytics (pricing, placement, optimization-style problems).

## Entrepreneurship, consulting, and influence (OAM)

- **OAM 236: Foundations of Entrepreneurship** — Entrepreneurial perspective and applied business basics (also open to many non-BBA paths).
- **OAM 436: Entrepreneurship** — Functional knowledge applied to starting and building ventures.
- **OAM 471: Applied Entrepreneurship** — Hands-on experience closest to starting and implementing a real business.
- **OAM 338: Consulting** / **OAM 438: Management Consulting** — Structured problem-solving used heavily in PM interviews and discovery.
- **OAM 432: Negotiations** — Stakeholder management and conflict skills for cross-functional influence.

## Finance that helps PMs

- **FIN 322: Impact: Strategic Valuation** — Assess a company’s strategic / potential value with financial and economic analysis.

## Recommended academic paths for PM

- **Marketing + ISOM / analytics** — Customer focus + technical literacy for tech PM roles.
- **Marketing + entrepreneurship** — Strong for 0→1 / innovation PM work.
- **Design Thinking (BUS 360) + MKT 347 + ISOM 352** — Empathy, product ownership, and data fluency in one stack.
- **Consulting (OAM 338/438) + Marketing** — Structured problem-solving plus customer focus.

## How to talk about these courses in PM interviews

- Tie **MKT 347 / BUS 360** to product sense and user discovery.
- Tie **ISOM 352 / MKT 342 / BUS 350** to metrics, experiments, and analytical interviews.
- Tie **MKT 443** to monetization and prioritization tradeoffs.
- Tie **MKT 442 / OAM 471 / OAM 438** to shipped outcomes, client work, and leadership stories (STAR).
