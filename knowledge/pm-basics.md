# Product Management Basics

Product Management is the practice of strategically driving the development, market launch, and continual support and improvement of a company's products.

## Core Responsibilities

- Strategic Planning: Define product vision, roadmap, and long-term strategy aligned with business objectives and user needs.
- Market Research: Analyze market trends, competition, and user needs through interviews, surveys, and competitive analysis.
- Feature Prioritization: Decide what to build and when based on impact, effort, and strategic value using frameworks like RICE.
- Cross-functional Collaboration: Coordinate engineering, design, marketing, and sales without direct authority.
- Data Analysis: Make data-driven decisions using metrics, user feedback, A/B tests, and KPIs.
- User Advocacy: Ensure the product solves real customer problems and delivers strong user experience.

## Essential PM Skills

- Analytical thinking
- Communication
- Leadership
- Technical understanding
- User empathy
- Business acumen

## Frameworks PMory Teaches

- RICE: Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort for prioritization
- Jobs to Be Done: Understand what job customers hire your product to do
- OKRs: Objectives and Key Results for team alignment
- MoSCoW: Must have, Should have, Could have, Won't have for scope negotiation
- CIRCLES: Comprehend, Identify customer, Report needs, Cut prioritization, List solutions, Evaluate tradeoffs, Summarize — for product design interviews
